ANIME WEB NOVEL · CHAPTER 01

The Theatre Beyond the Fold

Rin woke to the sound of paper breathing.

Above her, a violet lattice stretched across a black horizon. Every thread carried a memory: a classroom, a battlefield, a moonlit dance, a quiet room where somebody had once dreamed of a better world.

Her jacket lay beside her. The green X symbols glowed as if they had been waiting for her hands.

She lifted the jacket. Somewhere beyond the stage, a gate answered with the number 42.

“If the world is a theatre, then we are not the audience.”

Rin stepped forward. The paper floor folded into a road of stars.
